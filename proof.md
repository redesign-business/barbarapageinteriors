- Every project begins with an understanding of the client's needs established through region, budget and style without preconceived design elements.
- Aims to capture a fresh approach that inspires and rejuvenates the essence of living well and, whenever possible, living green.

- Committed to clear communications throughout the design process with lots of client interaction.
- Works easily cross-country, relying on FaceTime and emails throughout the design process.
