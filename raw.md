Barbara Page Interiors is a design firm dedicated to architecture, interiors and design. Since Barbara has lived in South Lake Tahoe all her life, she has a keen sense of design which enhances the natural beauty of Lake Tahoe. With many years of experience in the design industry Barbara has a sophisticated eye and a knack for timeless design styles.

Since opening in 1999, Barbara Page Interiors’ work can be characterized by their passion for creating exceptional, pure and stylish environments for both contemporary and classic design. Project types include residential design with a focus on dwellings and vacation homes. Projects consist of new construction, renovations and additions.

Barbara Page Interiors approaches all projects according to the individual client serving their needs and lifestyles above all else. Every project begins with an understanding of the client’s needs established through region, budget and style without preconceived design elements. The firm aims to capture a fresh approach that inspires and rejuvenates the essence of living well and whenever possible living green.

Barbara Page Interiors strives to offer exceptional service and attention to details with agreed meetings and conferencing. The firm is strongly committed to clear communications throughout the process of design with lots of client interaction. The goals and project requirements are all details that are important to the firm’s dedication to the overall satisfaction of their client.

Past projects have encompassed both national and international work with an emphasis on the west coast, Chicago, Florida and Mexico. Barbara Page Interiors has been recognized by the designindustry being published in Architectural Digest Magazine as well as having projects recognized on HGTV Homes across America. Projects have also been featured in regional home design magazines. Barbara Page Interiors has also been recognized as part of the design team for the Presidential Inaugural Award Committee and Academy Awards Production Department.

PHILIPPE STARCK

[FLOS- Philippe Stark Lighting](https://usa.flos.com/brands/Philippe-Starck.html)

Philippe Starck was born in Paris in 1949. He graduated from the École Missim de Camondo in 1967 and founded his first design firm in 1968.

In 1982, Philippe was commissioned by the then-president of France, Francois Mitterrand, to design the interior space for the private apartments in the Elysée Palace. Following that incredible achievement, he designed the famous Café Costes, cementing his position in the design world.

Philippe also designed the École Nationale Supérieure des Arts Décoratifs in Paris and the control tower at Bordeaux airport.

Philippe believes that objects should “good” before being beautiful. The designer also believes in the concept of “democratic design,” meaning that quality objects should be available at lower prices so that more people can enjoy the best. He considers it his duty to share his ethical and subversive vision of a more fair world. The designer has created a wide range of objects from furniture to mail order homes, motorbikes, mega-yachts, and even artistic direction for space-travel projects.

We would like to wish you all a Happy Holiday season from all of us at BPI and in doing so share one of our favorite holiday recipes with you. This fudge will fill your home interiors and senses with delicious feelings of Christmas!

![](https://barbarapageinteriors.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/dec-2-282x300.png)

Eggnog Fudge

½ cup butter

¾ cup eggnog

2 cups white sugar

2 cups white chocolate chips

1-7oz jar marshmallow crème

2 tsp. vanilla extract

½ tsp. nutmeg (grated)

1.  Line a 9×9 pan with foil. Spray with non-stick cooking spray.
2.  In a medium sauce pan, combine butter, sugar, and eggnog. Bring to a boil.
3.  Cook until the temperature reaches 234 degrees on a candy thermometer.
4.  Remove from heat. Stir in white chocolate until melted.
5.  Stir in marshmallow crème, vanilla, and nutmeg.
6.  Evenly spread into prepared pan.
7.  Garnish with more grated nutmeg if desired.

This private residence features design influences from Berman Rosetti, Altura Furniture and Holly Hunt, to name a few. Take a look below to see what styles we are loving this season!
